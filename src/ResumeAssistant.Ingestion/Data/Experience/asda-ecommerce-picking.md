# Technical Ownership & Platform Engineering for UK Grocery Fulfilment

Served as Technical Owner for a major UK retailer's Azure-based eCommerce picking platform supporting **700,000+ weekly customer orders across 600+ stores**. Accountable for platform strategy, architecture, delivery, and cross-functional collaboration with enterprise architects, product managers, and engineering leaders.

## Key Responsibilities & Leadership
- Designed and evolved a scalable, event-driven Azure architecture aligned with the **Azure Well-Architected Framework**, enabling resilient nationwide grocery fulfilment.
- Led end-to-end engineering delivery across solution architecture, software development in C#/.NET, Terraform-based Infrastructure as Code (IaC), DevSecOps, and production operations.
- Improved developer experience (DevEx) by introducing shared Terraform modules, Azure DevOps templates, and GitHub Actions workflows, actively promoting InnerSource engineering practices across teams.
- Established rigorous engineering standards for observability (Azure Monitor, Application Insights), deployment pipelines, and platform architecture, driving consistency and operational resilience.
- Ensured reliable, high-performance platform operations during major peak flash events including **90,000+ orders in 30 minutes** and **150,000+ Christmas orders** while guiding ongoing platform evolution and strategic roadmap direction.
