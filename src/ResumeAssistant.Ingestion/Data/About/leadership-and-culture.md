# Engineering Leadership, Culture & Architecture Governance

As a Principal Engineer and Solutions Architect, my leadership philosophy centres on technical enablement — empowering autonomous engineering teams, establishing rigorous but lightweight architectural guardrails, and fostering a culture of continuous learning and operational excellence.

## Technical Ownership & Architecture Governance
- **Architecture Decision Records (ADR):** Established and facilitated lightweight, iterative ADR processes across delivery squads, ensuring every significant design decision is documented, transparent, and traceable to a business rationale.
- **Architecture Review Boards (ARB):** Part of periodic architecture review sessions to evaluate proposed designs against the Azure Well-Architected Framework's five pillars (Reliability, Security, Cost Optimization, Operational Excellence, Performance Efficiency).
- **Technical Risk Management:** Introduced structured spike-and-prototype cycles before committing to irreversible architectural decisions, reducing mid-sprint rework and technical debt accumulation.
- **Production Ownership Model:** Championed a "you build it, you run it" culture where delivery squads own their services end-to-end from development through production monitoring, incident response, and post-mortem reviews.

## Stakeholder Management & Cross-Functional Collaboration
- **Executive & Product Bridge:** Served as the primary technical bridge between product management, executive sponsors, enterprise security teams, infrastructure operations, and hands-on software engineers — translating business intent into actionable technical roadmaps.
- **Microsoft FastTrack Partnership:** Collaborated directly with Microsoft FastTrack engineers and client enterprise architects to align cloud modernisation strategies, governance guardrails, and migration pathways across large multi-team programmes.
- **Multi-Vendor Coordination:** Coordinated technical delivery across distributed teams spanning multiple vendors and geographies (UK, Belgium, India), establishing shared engineering standards and communication protocols.
- **Incident Command:** Led cross-functional war-room coordination during peak trading periods, ensuring rapid triage, clear ownership chains, and structured post-incident reviews with blameless retrospectives.

## Mentorship, Coaching & Technical Enablement
- **Engineering Mentorship:** Mentored senior and lead engineers in system design, distributed systems trade-offs, cloud-native architecture principles, and career progression from mid-level to senior/principal tracks.
- **AI & Modern .NET Upskilling:** Hosted technical workshops, lunch-and-learns, and hands-on hackathons on Agentic AI, Microsoft Agent Framework, Model Context Protocol (MCP), Anthropic Claude, and modern .NET 10 patterns — accelerating team adoption of emerging technologies.
- **Code Review Culture:** Established and modelled thorough, constructive code review practices emphasising knowledge transfer, design intent discussion, and incremental improvement over gate-keeping.
- **Community Contributions:** Published technical deep dives on distributed architectures, Azure event-driven systems, and AI engineering on [anktsrkr.github.io](https://anktsrkr.github.io), contributing to the broader .NET and cloud engineering community.
